# Privacy policy

[Leer en español](PRIVACY.es.md)

DFX Photo Widget doesn't collect, store or share any personal data. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

- **No network.** The app doesn't have the Internet permission, so it can't send anything anywhere.
- **Your photos stay on your phone.** The app only reads the folders and photos you choose,
  through Android's own folder picker and photo picker. It doesn't ask for general storage access.
- **What it saves, and where.** Each widget's settings (the chosen folders and photos, interval
  and appearance) and a list of the photos to show are kept in the app's private storage on your
  phone. They are excluded from Android backups. Removing a widget deletes its data and releases
  the permissions it no longer needs. Uninstalling the app deletes everything.
- **Diagnostic log.** The app keeps a short technical log (widget sizes, errors, when photos are
  loaded; never the photos themselves) in the same private storage, limited to 512 KB. It only
  leaves your phone if you save a diagnostic report yourself and decide to share it.
- **No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no third-party libraries that collect data.**

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/davefx/dfx-photowidget/issues
